❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I start with Community and upgrade later?

Absolutely! Your project files are 100% compatible across all editions. Simply activate a license key and all premium features will be unlocked instantly.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

You can continue using the Community Edition with all your existing project files. Premium features will be disabled, but your data and configurations remain intact.

Is the source code really included?

Yes! Community Edition is MIT-licensed with full source code on GitHub. Professional includes view and modification rights. Enterprise includes full access and customization rights.

Do you offer education or non-profit discounts?

Yes! Educational institutions get 50% off Professional, and we offer free Enterprise for teaching labs. Non-profits receive 30% off. Contact education@aicorehmi.com to apply.

Can I run AI Core HMI offline?

Yes! Use Ollama for local AI, and Enterprise customers can activate licenses offline for air-gapped deployments.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards, ACH, wire transfer, and purchase orders (Enterprise only).
