# Sponsorship & Funding

Thank you for considering supporting AI Core HMI! Your sponsorship helps us maintain and improve the platform.

---

## 🌟 Why Sponsor?

AI Core HMI is **free and open source** (MIT license), but development, infrastructure, and support require resources. Your sponsorship helps:

- ✅ **Faster development** of new features and bug fixes
- ✅ **Better documentation** and tutorials
- ✅ **Community support** infrastructure (Discord, forums)
- ✅ **Free hosting** of demo instances and documentation
- ✅ **Security audits** and dependency updates
- ✅ **Conference talks** and ecosystem growth
