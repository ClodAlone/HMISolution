## 🚨 Priority Definitions

### P1 - Critical
**Complete loss of service or critical functionality**

**Examples:**
- Server won't start in production
- Data loss or corruption
- Security breach
- System-wide crashes

**SLA:**
- Professional: 4 business hours initial response
- Enterprise: 1 hour initial response, 24x7

**Resolution Target:**
- Professional: 24 hours or workaround
- Enterprise: 8 hours or workaround

---

### P2 - High
**Severe degradation of service**

**Examples:**
- Performance issues affecting operations
- Major feature not working
- Intermittent crashes
- Alarms not triggering

**SLA:**
- Professional: 8 business hours initial response
- Enterprise: 4 hours initial response

**Resolution Target:**
- Professional: 3 business days
- Enterprise: 48 hours

---

### P3 - Medium
**Minor functionality issue**

**Examples:**
- Non-critical feature bug
- UI/UX issues
- Documentation errors
- Driver configuration problems

**SLA:**
- Professional: 2 business days
- Enterprise: 1 business day

**Resolution Target:**
- Professional: 2 weeks or next release
- Enterprise: 1 week or hotfix

---

### P4 - Low
**Cosmetic issues, feature requests**

**Examples:**
- Enhancement requests
- Documentation improvements
- "How-to" questions
- Best practice guidance

**SLA:**
- Professional: 5 business days
- Enterprise: 2 business days

**Resolution Target:**
- Professional: Roadmap consideration
- Enterprise: Prioritized roadmap

## 📝 SLA Exclusions

SLAs do not apply when issues are caused by:
- Third-party software failures
- Network/infrastructure issues outside AI Core HMI
- Improper configuration or misuse
- Running unsupported versions
- Hardware failures
- Force majeure events
